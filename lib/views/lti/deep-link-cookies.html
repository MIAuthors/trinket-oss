Your browser is blocking cookies for {{ siteName }}

{{ siteName }} is running inside a window owned by your LMS. Your browser treats it as a third party here and is withholding the sign-in it just issued, so this page cannot tell who you are — even though you may be signed in to {{ siteName }} in another tab.

To choose content for this assignment, allow cookies for this site only:

Chrome
  1. Look in the address bar of this LMS page for the eye or cookie icon (it appears when cookies are being blocked), and choose to allow third-party cookies for the site.
  2. Or, more permanently: Settings → Privacy and security → Third-party cookies → Sites allowed to use third-party cookies → Add, and enter {% if siteHost %}[*.]{{ siteHost }}{% else %}this site's address{% endif %}.
    Note the difference: the address-bar control applies to the LMS page you are on, while this list is keyed on {{ siteName }}'s own address.
  3. Reload, then launch the assignment setup again from your LMS.

In an Incognito window Chrome blocks third-party cookies by default — an ordinary window may be all that is needed.

Safari

Settings → Privacy, and turn off Prevent cross-site tracking while you set up the assignment.

Firefox

Click the shield in the address bar of this LMS page and turn off Enhanced Tracking Protection for this site.

If your institution manages this setting and you cannot change it, you are not doing anything wrong — this needs your IT administrator, or a {{ siteName }} administrator who can set the assignment up outside the LMS window. Please report it: we would rather fix this than have you change a privacy setting.
